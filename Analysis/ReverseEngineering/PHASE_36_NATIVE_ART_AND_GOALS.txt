- Các tên sprite được giữ lại trong Unity/ObstacleArt/manifest.json cùng path_id, bundle và SHA-256.
- Các SpriteAtlas native đã được mở trực tiếp; danh sách packed sprite, path_id, kích thước và SHA-256
  nằm trong Unity/ObstacleArt/atlas-manifest.json.

  Gem, MagicOrb, Magnet, Cannon, Lily, Volcano, Lightbulb, Armory, JellyMaker, Tablet, MagicGlobe,

DRAGONSMALLBOX ATLAS FRAMES
- The native bundle contains a separate Spine texture `dragon_box` (Texture2D path_id 791, 780x1437), with source atlas text `Unity/TextAssets/85/1909_dragon_box.atlas`.
- The `yatay` and `dikey` frames were cropped from the atlas and added as Unity obstacle art: `dragon_box_yatay_2`, `dragon_box_yatay1_2`, `dragon_box_dikey_2`, and `dragon_box_dikey1_2`.
- Native enum-only variants are mapped by ID: 10002/10003 use horizontal frames and 10000/10001 use vertical frames. The frame-stage pairing follows the atlas frame names and the recovered tile converter; the final animation stage still depends on runtime confirmation.

2300 LEVEL RUNTIME ADAPTER
- All 2,300 numeric level envelopes in `Levels/1.json` through `Levels/2300.json` now have compact Unity TextAsset adapters under `Assets/Resources/RecoveredLevels/level_0001.json` through `level_2300.json`.
- The adapters preserve dimensions, inactive cells, native obstacle tile IDs, color goals, moves, refill queues and initial special-item powers. Native Set/Fill placeholder IDs are resolved to deterministic match colors because they do not carry a fixed color in the exported level grid.
- `CrownGame` loads the recovered adapter for the current level during normal PLAY progression and caps progression at level 2300. This makes every recovered level loadable in the offline runtime; exact native RNG, diagonal fall and all goal-specific effects remain separate fidelity work.

CELL OVERLAY PRESERVATION
- Recovered adapters now preserve the native Grid.Cells overlays independently from Grid.Items: Ice, HasJelly, Ivy, Curtain, Chain, Border and Puzzle.
- This permits combinations in one level and on the same cell. Level 6 now retains 9 Box3 cells plus 32 Ice cells, matching its native goals.
- CrownGame renders these overlays behind item art; MatchBoard clears their layers when a match or power reaches the cell and includes uncleared gameplay overlays in recovered-level completion.
- A scan of all 2,300 adapters found 10,471 Ice, 861 Jelly, 6,440 Ivy, 5,642 Curtain, 1,960 Chain, 3,463 Border and 1,278 Puzzle cells. All overlay arrays are width*height sized.
- TiledId 29 (Lyre) is also retained as a native static obstacle instead of being converted to a random match color. TiledId 40/50 remain pre-placed special items through the existing Dynamite/ElectroBall mapping.
