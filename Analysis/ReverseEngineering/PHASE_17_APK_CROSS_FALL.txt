PHASE 17 - APK CROSS-FALL CHECK
Date: 2026-11-12

APK evidence
- Native FallManager.ManualUpdate order is: ClearTargetItems -> CalculateStraightFalls -> CalculateCrossFalls -> CallFallUpdates -> AddTopBoardRemainingItemsToQueue.
- CellPoint exposes eight neighbor offsets, including the two downward diagonals.
- CalculateCrossFalls invokes FindCrossItem, which searches the left/right adjacent columns.
- IsFallPossibleFromCellToCell rejects a blocked source and delegates to the item's CanFallCross check.
- FindTargetItemFromCrossCell additionally rejects blocked/frozen targets and already-reserved destinations.

Meaning
- A jewel can fall diagonally into a lower empty region in the neighboring column after straight compression.
- It is not an arbitrary horizontal slide across a row, and it cannot pass through a blocker or let two sources claim one destination.

Prototype change
- MatchBoard.Collapse now follows straight fall -> cross-fall -> top refill, matching the native update order.
- Cross-fall checks the adjacent columns bottom-up, moves a fallable jewel one diagonal step into an empty lower cell, records its source in the movement map, and clears the source before the next reservation.
- Refill remains limited to segments connected to the top; holes below boxes/walls/columns remain isolated.

Validation
- Added a test with a wall blocking one column and a two-item source in the adjacent column; the lower empty segment receives the diagonal source item.
- Unity staging build passed: 22616 assertions, 4500 legal actions, 6640 cascade steps.
- Static native evidence remains limited for item-specific CanFallCross virtual implementations and deeper multi-step arbitration; the port implements the verified one-step adjacent-column behavior with reservation.
